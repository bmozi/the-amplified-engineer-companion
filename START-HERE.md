# Start Here

The shortest useful path is:

`NAME THE WORK → SET THE BOUNDARY → TRY → INSPECT → DECIDE`

## A fifteen-minute first use

1. Pick one reversible task whose result you can inspect.
2. Complete the task boundary in the
   [`Almost-Right Tax Ledger`](resources/almost-right-tax-ledger.md).
3. Try the task with or without AI.
4. Record work across generation, inspection, repair, coordination, recovery,
   and transfer. Include work shifted to another person.
5. Choose a disposition: repeat, revise, narrow, keep human-led, or stop.

The goal is not to prove that AI is good or bad. The goal is to see what this
way of working changed and what evidence should govern the next attempt.

## If an AI system can act

Before the system reads sensitive context, calls a tool, edits a file, sends a
message, or creates another side effect, complete the
[`Agent Action Safety Check`](resources/agent-action-safety-check.md). If you
cannot name the authority, evidence, stop condition, and recovery path, keep
the system advisory.

## Completion standard

A useful session leaves at least one durable result:

- a clearer requirement or question;
- a narrower authority boundary;
- evidence that changed the decision;
- a specific control or recovery path;
- a lesson another person can reuse.

A long transcript or a high volume of generated output is not, by itself,
evidence of amplification.
