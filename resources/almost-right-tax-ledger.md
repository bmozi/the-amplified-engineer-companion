## Diagnose before deciding

- Which missing or contradictory context mattered?
- Which decision was hidden inside the request?
- Where was authority broader than intended?
- Which evidence shared the candidate's assumption?
- At what point should the work have stopped?
- Would a smaller task, different tool, manual approach, or stronger
  environment change the result?
- What did the organization learn that another person can now reuse?

## Disposition

Choose one and explain why:

- repeat under the same boundary;
- repeat with changed context, tool, evidence, or authority;
- narrow the work class;
- keep the task human led;
- stop using this path until the environment changes.

The best disposition is the one supported by the complete work, not the one
that produces the most impressive adoption story.
