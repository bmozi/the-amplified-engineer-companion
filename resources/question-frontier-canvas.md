# Question Frontier Canvas

Use this before asking AI for a recommendation or authorizing an agent to act.
The purpose is to identify questions whose answers could change the decision,
not to produce the longest possible checklist.

## 1. Decision

What decision must be made? Name the choice, consequence owner, affected
system, and time horizon.

> We must decide whether _________________________________________________
> before __________________ because ______________________________________.

## 9. Reversal conditions

- We will reject or pause the preferred approach if:

## Optional AI starting instruction

> Do not solve this problem yet. Help me construct the question frontier for
> the decision. First restate the decision and distinguish facts I supplied
> from your inferences. Then map prerequisite concepts, hidden premises,
> affected stakeholders, lifecycle consequences, credible alternatives, and
> evidence that would distinguish among them. For each proposed question,
> explain why its answer could change the decision. Identify questions you can
> explore from the supplied sources and questions that require a domain expert,
> stakeholder, experiment, or authoritative external source. Rank the first
> five questions by consequence and information value. Ask them one at a time.

## Completion test

The canvas is useful when it changes at least one boundary, sequence, evidence
requirement, stakeholder conversation, or stop condition. It has failed if it
only produces a longer list while leaving the decision untouched.
