# Thought-Partner Session Guide

Use this when you want AI to improve your thinking rather than simply produce
an answer.

## Prepare

- **Decision or learning goal:**
- **What I currently believe:**
- **Why I believe it:**
- **Where I am uncertain:**
- **Who or what could be affected:**
- **Approved context:**
- **Facts or data I must not disclose:**
- **Evidence that could change my mind:**

## Select one conversation job

Choose one job for the current stage.

- [ ] Orient: map vocabulary, prerequisites, and major frames.
- [ ] Interview: ask one question at a time to expose intent and assumptions.
- [ ] Teach: begin in ordinary language, show a concrete case and analogy, name
  the analogy's limit, restore precise vocabulary, give a counterexample, and
  test my understanding through prediction and teach-back.
- [ ] Generate alternatives: produce meaningfully different options.
- [ ] Challenge: make the strongest case against my preferred view.
- [ ] Rehearse: simulate a difficult review or stakeholder conversation.
- [ ] Synthesize: organize evidence and unresolved disagreements.

## Protect active reasoning

Select at least one constraint.

- [ ] Withhold the answer until I make a prediction.
- [ ] Ask one question at a time.
- [ ] Ask me to explain the concept back before correcting me.
- [ ] Begin with ELI5, then require precise vocabulary and the analogy's limit.
- [ ] Let me rank the alternatives before showing your ranking.
- [ ] Ask for my failure hypothesis before generating yours.
- [ ] Label supplied facts, inferences, and unknowns separately.

## Expand

Generate the relevant vocabulary, questions, stakeholders, mechanisms,
alternatives, analogies, and possible consequences. Do not rank yet.

## Apply pressure

- Which ideas depend on the same premise?
- What is the strongest counterexample to each serious approach?
- Which affected perspective is absent?
- What happens during partial failure, recovery, transfer, or retirement?
- Which evidence would discriminate among the options?
- What would make the preferred approach wrong?

## Close with a learning receipt

- **What changed my mind:**
- **What I can now explain or do:**
- **Generated claims that remain unverified:**
- **External source, experiment, or person needed next:**
- **Different problem where I will test transfer:**
- **Artifact or shared practice where I will preserve the lesson:**

## Completion test

The session created value if it changed a question, decision, experiment,
stakeholder conversation, or durable artifact. A long transcript alone is not
evidence of learning.
