# AI Leverage Next-Step Guide

This guide is a mirror, not a test. It does not assign a level, compare people,
or prescribe maximum AI use. Complete it for one real task in one real
environment. Your answers may change for another task tomorrow.

## 1. Name the work

- What outcome matters?
- Who uses or experiences it?
- What could go wrong if the result is persuasive but incorrect?
- How observable and reversible is the work?

## 2. Describe your present posture

Choose any statements that fit. More than one can be true.

- I have a principled reason not to use AI for this work.
- A prior failure reduced my trust, and that cost has not been repaired.
- I am curious but do not yet have a safe way to try.
- I use AI for pieces of the work while deliberately practicing implementation
  myself.
- I receive useful results and can usually verify them.
- I define intent, authority, stopping conditions, evidence, and recovery.
- I help other people learn and improve the shared environment.

Then write: **The experience that most shaped this posture was...**

Do not skip this sentence. A posture without its history invites judgment. Its
history often reveals the support that is actually needed.

Continued non-use is a valid outcome. The experiment succeeds when it improves
your knowledge, not when it increases adoption.

## 5. Review without shame

After the attempt, answer:

1. Where did the tool reduce effort or expand reach?
2. Where did it move effort onto review, repair, explanation, or another
   person?
3. Which judgment became more important?
4. What did you understand afterward that you did not understand before?
5. What would you repeat, narrow, or stop?
6. What must the team or environment provide before the next step is safe?

Finish with one sentence:

> For this kind of work, my next responsible step is...

That sentence is the output. It is not a maturity score and should not be used
as one.
