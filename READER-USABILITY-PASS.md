# External Reader Usability Pass

## Status

Recruitment is open for companion release **v1.0.0**. Automated validation
checks structure and links; it is not represented as human reader evidence.

## Purpose

Determine whether a new reader can find the right entry point, choose one
bounded AI-assisted task, use the appropriate worksheet, and leave with a
decision or evidence record without coaching.

## Small-pass protocol

Recruit three to five readers who resemble the intended audience. Include at
least one practicing engineer and one engineering leader. When available,
include one reader who routinely uses keyboard navigation, text reflow,
magnification, or a screen reader.

Do not introduce the repository beyond this sentence: “Use this companion to
apply one idea from *The Amplified Engineer* to a real, low-consequence task.”
Then ask each reader to perform these tasks:

1. Find the intended starting point and explain what to do first.
2. Choose one low-consequence task and locate the worksheet that carries it
   through the book.
3. Find the check used before an AI agent receives context, tools, or authority.
4. Use the Almost-Right Tax Ledger to name one hidden cost of a plausible but
   incorrect result.
5. Explain where human judgment remains necessary in the chosen exercise.
6. Download the stable offline release and locate the same worksheet in it.
7. Find how to report an error, security concern, or accessibility barrier.

Record completion, elapsed time, wrong turns, questions, and the reader's own
words. Do not rescue the reader until a task has clearly failed; the point is
to find the seam, not to demonstrate the repository.

## Pass criteria

- At least four of five readers, or all three in a three-reader pass, find the
  starting point within two minutes.
- At least 80 percent of tasks are completed without coaching.
- Every reader can explain why a completed worksheet is evidence for a
  decision, not proof that an AI result is correct or safe.
- No critical accessibility barrier prevents completion of tasks 2, 6, or 7.
- Every repeated wrong turn becomes an issue with an owner and disposition.

Submit one issue per participant using the
[reader-usability form](https://github.com/bmozi/the-amplified-engineer-companion/issues/new?template=reader-usability.yml).
Aggregate conclusions only after the small pass is complete; do not average
away a severe failure.
